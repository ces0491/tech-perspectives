I wanted to know where to buy firewood most cheaply in Cape Town, so I built a site that checks the vendors' online shops every morning and ranks what they sell by rand per kilogram. Working alone in my spare time, five years ago that would have taken me the better part of a year, and I'd never have started.

In September I also released a plugin for Claude Code, Anthropic's coding tool. Two weeks later Anthropic changed Claude Code's default model, and in October it added a warning that did another of the plugin's jobs. There isn't enough left in it now for anyone to bother installing it.

Both come from building getting cheap. That's great when I'm the one who needs the tool. It also makes what I build for anyone else less likely to add much, because someone better placed may well get there first.
